Image resize, crop, and quality comparison lets you upload or paste a photo, drag a crop box (including past the image edges to add padding), and generate preview versions at full and half width across several quality levels for JPEG, WebP, AVIF, or PNG. Each preview shows its dimensions, quality, and file size, can be toggled between normal and full-width display, and downloads directly. The background color is detected from the image edges and can be changed manually or made transparent for formats that support alpha, and WebP and AVIF fall back to WebAssembly encoders when the browser cannot encode them natively.
