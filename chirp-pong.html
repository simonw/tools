CHIRPPONG

Two phones. One ball.
Carried between them by sound.

First to

No Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or accounts. The two phones talk to each other by chirping, using the speaker and microphone.

Pair the phones

  1. Open this page on both phones and tap Play on two phones.
  2. Turn the volume all the way up (and switch off silent mode).
  3. Lay the phones on a table top-to-top, a few centimetres apart.
  4. Tap Call on one phone. The other one answers by itself.

How to play

Each phone is one half of the court. The top edge of your screen is a portal: the ball flies through it, the phone chirps it across, and it bursts out of the top of the other phone. You'll hear it go.

CONTROLS

  • Drag anywhere to move your paddle. It floats just above your finger.
  • Hit with the paddle's edge to angle your shot.
  • Swipe sideways as you hit to add spin. The ball curves, and keeps curving on their screen.
  • Flick up into the ball to SMASH it.

HEAT

Long rallies heat up the court. 6+ hits and the point is worth ×2, 12+ and it's worth ×3.

BOMBS

Every return charges your bomb meter. When it's full, tap while the ball is on their side to drop a bomb onto their screen, lined up with where you tapped. If it hits their paddle, the paddle is stunned.

WINNING

Miss the ball and they score. Whoever misses serves next.
