Analyze text for cliché and formulaic phrasing commonly found in AI-generated writing. Paste a draft, article, or chat transcript into the page, or load content from a URL via the Jina Reader proxy; the tool scans for repeated patterns such as "no X, no Y" chains, "you already know," "let's be clear," and other phrases catalogued in Wikipedia's Signs of AI writing guide. The app groups findings by pattern family, highlights offending sentences in context, and shows a badge count for chain-style matches so you can see how many items are in a repeated list.

Use the pattern panel to enable or disable specific detection categories, click a match in the list to jump to the corresponding sentence, and toggle "Show just the highlights" to focus on the flagged text. The page also includes an example loader, clear button, and browser-only localStorage persistence so your settings remain available between sessions. Everything runs client-side in the browser with no backend required.
