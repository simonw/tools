Quick Study paints a photo stroke by stroke in passes from coarse brushes to fine ones, and accepts images from a file picker, drag and drop, or the clipboard. Users choose a medium (oil, watercolor, or pointillist), a detail level, and a duration of 2 to 10 seconds, then hold a button to compare against the original photo, repaint with a new random take, or download the finished study as a PNG through the Downloads service when available or a plain file link otherwise. The status line tracks the current pass, brush size, stroke count, and finish time, while a progress bar and caption show the source name, medium, and dimensions.
