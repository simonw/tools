    Field Guide

    How to play

    Every round grows a pair of trees from one seed. They match in almost every way, but a few things differ: a missing branch, a bird in one and not the other, a bough that turned early. Tap a difference in either tree to notice it.

    Looking

    Drag to turn both trees together. Some differences only show from behind. Pinch or scroll to look closer, and drag with two fingers (or right-click) to move the view. Reset view puts things back.

    Scoring

    Each difference is worth 100, plus a bonus for noticing quickly and for runs of finds. Taps that hit nothing cost a little. A hint points the way; pairs finished without one, and with few misses, earn all three leaves. After every third pair, a Connoisseur's round asks you to judge two different trees by eye.

    Keys

    ← → turn, ↑ ↓ tilt, + − zoom, H hint, R reset view, M sound, Enter next pair.
